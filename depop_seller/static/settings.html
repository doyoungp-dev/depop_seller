Anthropic API key checking…

Pays for grouping photos into items - the one step that uses the paid API, about $2.50 for 440 photos. Descriptions do not use it. The key is kept in a file on this computer (), never sent anywhere but Anthropic, and is never shown back to you in full.
No key yet? Make one at console.anthropic.com → API keys → Create key, and add some credit. Paste the whole thing, including sk-ant-.

Claude sign-in checking…

Writes the descriptions, on your own Claude subscription (Pro or Max) - no API charge. You sign in once, in your browser.
Get the Claude app
A Claude page has opened in your browser. Sign in there and press Authorize - this page notices by itself when you are done.

Chrome helper checking…

Puts the photos and the description into Depop's form inside your own Chrome, so there is no separate login and nothing for Depop to block.
  1. Open a new tab at chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  2. Press Copy here, then click Load unpacked, paste into the Folder box at the bottom of the window that opens, and press Select Folder:
  3. Come back here and press Reload.

Updates checking…

New versions of Depop Seller are published on GitHub. The app only looks when you press the button. Updating replaces the app itself and reopens it - your photos, listings, style and key are never touched.

Close the app

Closing the app window closes the app - nothing keeps running in the background. If it is still grouping photos, writing a description or feeding the Depop helper, it stays up until that finishes. This button stops it right now.

Your data folder

Everything of yours lives in one folder, separate from the app: your photos and their grouping, your listings and descriptions, your style, your API key. To use Depop Seller on another computer, install the app there and copy this one folder across - nothing else.

Where things are

Everything runs on this computer. Nothing is uploaded except the photos you group (to Anthropic) and what you post yourself (to Depop).
