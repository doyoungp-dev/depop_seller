Depop opens Depop's List an item page in a new tab of this browser, and the helper extension adds the item's photos there in the order shown (photo 1 = cover). Nothing is posted: you fill in the rest and press Post yourself. If Depop asks you to log in first, the photos are added as soon as the form appears. Drag a photo to change the order (saved to the manifest at once); click one to see it large. Tick Listed when you've posted an item - listed items move to the bottom of the page. Descriptions: type the facts you'd normally give Claude (brand, size, condition, flaws, measurements) in the small box and click Generate Description - or tick several items and use Generate Selected Descriptions at the top. The text follows your house style from the Style tab, and anything not known becomes a [placeholder]. Edit freely - the box is what gets pasted into Depop together with the photos. Descriptions are written by Claude on your own subscription, never the paid API.
One-time setup - install the helper extension (2 minutes). It lets this page add photos to Depop inside your own Chrome, so there is no separate login and nothing for Depop to block.
  1. Open a new tab at chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder .
  3. Come back here and click Reload. The buttons turn red when the helper is detected.
Until then the button falls back to opening a separate automated Chrome window, which Depop may block.
